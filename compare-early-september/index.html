About this data

This is a fixed archive of one past event, not a live view. It holds every three-hourly UKV run from Wed 2 Sep 00Z to Fri 4 Sep 00Z, each verified against the radar and gauge observations that actually followed. Step through a single run with the slider to see how it verified, or hold the time steady and change run to see how successive runs converged on the event as lead time shortened.

  • Radar: Met Office ODIM H5 1km composite, 15-minute observations as recorded at the time. British Crown copyright Met Office — CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • UKV forecast: Met Office UK Variable resolution model, ~1.5km grid. Only the three-hourly synoptic runs (00/03/06/09/12/15/18/21Z) are shown — the off-cycle hourly runs publish to T+11h only, too short to carry useful lead time. Re-rendered from the Met Office archive through the same code path the live page uses. British Crown copyright Met Office — CC BY-SA 4.0.
  • Rain gauges: Natural Resources Wales (NRW) and Environment Agency 15-minute tipping-bucket readings.
  • Catchment boundaries: National River Flow Archive (2014). NERC Environmental Information Data Centre. doi:10.5285/1957166d.
  • Region / county boundaries: Office for National Statistics (ONS). Open Government Licence.
